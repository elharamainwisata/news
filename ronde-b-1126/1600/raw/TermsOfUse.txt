Users wishing to perform Umrah and Ziyarah must obtain Umrah visas in advance. Please ensure you follow your visa requirements, and you can apply for an Umrah visa electronically when registering on the Nusuk Umrah platform.

It is not permitted to work in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia on a Umrah visa.
Saudi Arabia uses the Islamic calendar for all official matters. Make sure you know when your visa expires.
