The Ministry of Hajj and Umrah Announces New Features in the "Nusuk" App
In response to user suggestions from a survey  launched on the "Tafaul" platform targeting "Nusuk"  partners to gather their feedback and suggestions on adding new religious  services to enhance their experience and meet their needs, the Ministry of Hajj  and Umrah has announced the addition of a new feature to the Nusuk App that  allows users to accurately determine prayer times based on their location. This  feature received the highest level of support, with 53% of the 441 participants  in the survey, conducted through the ministry's official accounts on Twitter  and LinkedIn. The options presented included: prayer time service based on  location, prayer times according to the calendar, audio alerts for prayers, and  customizable Adhan voices. Based on the survey results, the ministry is  developing the selected new service, which is expected to be launched in the  first quarter of 2025 to provide the best experience for the guests of Allah.
