Can pilgrims perform Hajj every year, or is there a waiting period?
Priority is given to those who have not previously performed Hajj, except for a Mahram. This priority is applied only until the end of Shawwal. After that, booking will be available for anyone who meets the eligibility requirements for Hajj.
How can one apply for a Mahram exemption?
By selecting “Add Exemption Request” from the Actions button, completing the request form, and clicking Submit.
Is there a specific duration between submitting the exemption request and its approval?
There is no specific duration.
Can a woman perform Hajj without a Mahram?
Yes, a Mahram is not mandatory for all Hajj packages. Women can register under a women-only group.
Can I perform Hajj using a visit or work visa?
No, performing Hajj requires a valid national ID or residency permit.
What are the mandatory vaccinations for pilgrims?
Hajj requires the meningococcal vaccine. Seasonal influenza and COVID-19 vaccines are also recommended.
I performed Hajj last year. Can I perform Hajj this year with my wife, or on behalf of someone else?
Hajj is only allowed as a Mahram exemption for a female as a husband, brother, son, or father.
How can Gulf nationals residing inside the Kingdom, who are not residents, register for Hajj?
Citizens of GCC countries cannot register through this platform. Registration is only available for those holding a national ID or a residency permit.
How can one check eligibility for Hajj?
Hajj eligibility can be checked through the National Information Center.
What is the minimum age allowed for performing Hajj?
15 years for citizens and residents, based on the Gregorian date recorded on the national ID for citizens.The Gregorian date recorded in the passport applies to residents, with no upper age limit.
How can I check the status of my Hajj booking?
Upon completing the booking and making all required payments, the applicant will receive an immediate notification via SMS. Booking status can also be checked directly through the NUSUK Masar platform or the NUSUK app.
How can I find information about a Hajj establishment?
To view details of a Hajj establishment, select the “Hajj Establishments” button. A list of Hajj companies will be displayed. You can then choose the desired company and click on it to view all company details.
What is the payment mechanism and the due dates for this year?
The payment period for the booking invoice, from the start of bookings until 27/11/1447H, is 72 hours. If payment is not made within the specified period, the booking will be canceled. Starting from 28/11/1447H, the payment period for the booking invoice is reduced to 6 hours only.
What is the last date for choosing partial payment?
Partial payment is not available this year.
How are booking fees paid?
Booking fees are paid electronically only via the SADAD system.
Can I change the Hajj establishment after booking?
Changing the establishment after booking is not allowed. However, the booking can be canceled and rebooked if availability permits.
Are children allowed to accompany pilgrims with the companies?
Children are not allowed to accompany pilgrims.
Is it possible to purchase gifts and sacrificial animals through the platform?
Yes, but only after purchasing the Hajj package contract. Sacrificial animals can be purchased via the NUSUK app.
Is air travel included in the package, or does it have a different process this year?
Service providers can create a package that includes air travel. Pilgrims can also select packages dedicated to land transportation. Air travel can be requested through an airline service provider after completing the booking as an additional service.
Can air travel be added after completing the package booking?
Air travel can be requested separately through an airline service provider as an additional service via the NUSUK marketplace.
What is the procedure for canceling a Hajj booking?
Booking cancellations are made through the NUSUK Masar platform or the NUSUK app, as follows:
•
Before issuing the permit: If cancellation occurs by 30/10/1447H, no amount will be deducted.
•
If the permit is rejected by the Ministry of Interior: Electronic service fees of SAR 67.85 per pilgrim will be deducted.
•
If the permit has been issued: Cancellation from 1/11/1447H until the end of Dhu al-Qi’dah will incur a deduction of 10% of the contract value.
•
If the permit has been issued: Cancellation from 1/12/1447H until the platform closes will incur a deduction of 100% of the contract value.
Under what circumstances can a pilgrim get a refund?
Booking fees are refundable under the following conditions:
1.
Death of the main applicant, or of the husband in case of the wife.
2.
Traffic accidents preventing the performance of Hajj, with submission of proof confirming the information and dates.
3.
Criminal incidents preventing Hajj, with submission of proof confirming the information and dates.
4.
Health incapacity requiring hospitalization, supported by reports from government hospitals. If reports are from private hospitals, they must be certified by the Ministry of Health. All reports must be in Arabic.
When will Hajj permits start being issued through Absher?
Starting from 01/11/1447H.
Can I cancel my booking after the permit has been issued?
Yes, you can cancel via the NUSUK Masar platform or the NUSUK app by selecting “Cancel Booking,” in accordance with the booking cancellation policy.
How are refunds processed?
The refunded amount will be deposited into the account added by the beneficiary within 7 to 14 days from the cancellation date.
What types of Hajj packages are available this year, and what is the difference between them?
There are three Hajj packages available this year. Applicants can choose the package that suits them via the NUSUK app or website.
Are there additional services provided by Hajj establishments?
Yes, Hajj establishments have the right to offer optional additional services for their packages at extra cost. Pilgrims can select these services and pay for them through the Hajj program during the booking process.
How can I modify a booking, add companions, or assign a substitute?
Companions can be added or removed, or the entire booking can be canceled before finalizing the reservation. Individual bookings can also be canceled after completion.
How can sacrificial animals (Udhiyah) be purchased – if available?
Sacrificial animals can be purchased through the NUSUK app as an additional service, but only after purchasing the Hajj package contract.

What is the goal of Freedom of Information?
Freedom of Information aims to enable individuals to access available information from the Ministry of Hajj and Umrah in order to promote the principle of transparency and integrity.
What is Freedom of Information?
Freedom of Information is the right of individuals to access public data that the Ministry of Hajj and Umrah receives, produces, or handles after processing, regardless of its source, form, or nature.

Is purchasing transportation tickets mandatory? Is it required if there is a confirmed booking at a hotel near the Haram?

What are the assembly points and where are they located?
The main assembly points are located at the entrances to the Haram courtyards. They are the usual Haram checkpoints and do not have parking spaces. They are sorting points where it is verified that the pilgrim has an Umrah permit, then directed to the bus for transfer to the nearest point to the Haram. Assembly points provide a shuttle service to and from the Haram for those with confirmed bookings at one of the central hotels near the Haram.

COVID-19 - Meningococcal meningitis - Quadrivalent meningococcal vaccine (ACYW) at least 10 days before arriving in Hajj and Umrah areas - Seasonal influenza - Dose of Southern Hemisphere seasonal influenza vaccine
What are the most important registration requirements?
Priority in registration is given to those who have not performed Hajj before, excluding the Mahram. The national ID or residency must be valid until the end of Dhu al-Hijjah 1444. The allowed number of companions is 13. The mobile number used for booking cannot be used for another reservation. Companions wishing to perform Hajj must be registered in the same establishment and in a single package, using the "Add Companions" field during registration. The pilgrim’s health must be good and they must not suffer from severe chronic or infectious diseases.
What are the cases in which the Hajj booking amount is refunded?
Death, traffic and criminal accidents with submission of proof verifying the information and dates of occurrence, 2. Infection with COVID-19 after 01/12/1444H, after providing proof, and after the infected pilgrim cancels the booking first via the Absher portal then through the electronic Domestic Hajj path and submits a refund request, 3. Health incapacity or hospitalization, with submission of proof through reports issued by authorized authorities, 4. In case of updates to the current regulatory or health conditions that may conflict with the booking, the booking will be canceled and the full amount refunded to the account registered by the second party.
What is the cancellation policy?
Before payment, cancellation is free of charge. Before the permit is issued and until 14/10/1444 AH, the full contract amount will be refunded. If the permit issuance is rejected by the Ministry of Interior, an electronic service fee of 67.85 SAR will be deducted per pilgrim. After printing the permit from 15/10/1444 AH until the end of Dhu al-Qi'dah, 10% of the contract value will be deducted. After printing the permit from 01/12/1444 AH until the closure of the portal, 100% of the contract value will be charged.

My Umrah visa was rejected by the Saudi embassy in my country, what should I do?
Umrah visas fall under the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and its representations abroad. For inquiries, please contact the Ministry of Foreign Affairs at (920033334) from within the Kingdom or (+966920033334) from outside the Kingdom.
Is it possible to obtain an Umrah visa from one country and then travel from another country to the Kingdom to perform Umrah?
Yes, the pilgrim can obtain an Umrah visa from their country and travel from another country to the Kingdom to perform Umrah.
Can a resident Umrah pilgrim enter through the land ports with their own car or a rented car?
A resident Umrah pilgrim from countries authorized to enter the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia is allowed to enter through land ports with their own or rented car, provided they obtain a vehicle entry permit from the competent authorities in their country or the authorized agent only.
Can a pilgrim enter the Kingdom by one means of transport and exit by a different one?
Yes, that is possible.
Can I come with an Umrah visa without a service package?
An Umrah visa cannot be issued by the external agent without service packages (accommodation – transport).
Will I have to pay any additional amounts outside the contract I signed with the Umrah company in my country?
You are not required to pay any additional amounts outside the contract unless you wish to add other services not mentioned in the contract.
What is the latest date for Umrah pilgrims to leave without being considered in default by the Umrah companies?
According to the program through which they applied or before the visa period (90) days ends.
What is the Umrah visa fee for arrivals from outside Saudi Arabia?
There is a fee for issuing the Umrah visa, estimated at 300 Saudi Riyals.
Can Umrah pilgrims from abroad perform Umrah more than once in the same year?
Yes, it is possible to obtain an Umrah visa more than once in a year.
Is it possible to travel between cities in the Kingdom with an Umrah visa?
Yes, the Umrah visa allows the pilgrim to travel between Mecca, Medina, and all cities in the Kingdom during their permitted stay.
Is it possible to extend the Umrah visa for more than 90 days?
The system does not allow extending the Umrah visa for more than 90 days.
What is the duration of stay for arrivals with Umrah visas?
The duration of stay for arrivals with Umrah visas is 90 days.
How do I choose an Umrah company inside the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia?

How can you obtain an Umrah visa?
The visa can be obtained in two ways: designing the Umrah program directly through one of the approved electronic platforms (such as Nusk). Contacting the external agent to obtain the visa through the Nusk app, visiting the Ministry of Hajj and Umrah, selecting services, completing the requirements for issuing the visa, and booking the services. Can Umrah performers from abroad perform Umrah more than once during the same year?
Umrah visas start being issued from the 15th of Dhu al-Hijjah.
What is the Umrah visa?
An electronic visa granted by the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia to pilgrims wishing to perform Umrah, allowing them freedom of movement between Makkah, Madinah, and all cities of the Kingdom during their stay.
Is it possible to attend to perform Umrah one hour or more after the permit time?
We kindly ask you to adhere to the time specified in the permit to perform Umrah, and you may book again after the permit time has ended.
Is it possible to modify the Umrah time?
There is no feature to modify Umrah times, but you can cancel the appointment through the Nusk app before the permit time begins and issue a new permit. If the permit time has started, a new permit can be issued after the first permit expires.
Is it possible to enter the Haram before the permit time?
Entering the Grand Mosque for Umrah is linked to the permit time, but entering for prayer is allowed at any time.
Is there a specific time period between one Umrah and another?
The period between Umrah permits has been set so that only one active permit exists, and booking can be made again after the permit expires.
Is it allowed for children to be present in the courtyards of Al-Masjid an-Nabawi?
Yes, children are allowed to be present in the courtyards of Al-Masjid an-Nabawi.
Is it possible to modify or cancel the prayer time in the Rawdah Sharifah?
There is no feature to modify prayer times in the Rawdah Sharifah, but appointments can be canceled before the permit time through the Nusk app and a new permit can be issued.
Is it required to book a permit to pray and visit the Prophet’s grave (peace be upon him)?
Booking is not required, noting that sending salutations to the Prophet (peace be upon him) is allowed for men only.
Is there a specific time interval between visits to the Rawdah Sharifah?
Yes, 30 days
Is it required to book a permit to pray in the Rawdah Sharifah?
Yes, it is required to book a permit to pray in the Rawdah Sharifah.
Is it required to book a permit to pray in Al-Masjid an-Nabawi?
It is not required to book a time to pray in Al-Masjid an-Nabawi, but booking is required for prayer in the Rawdah Sharifah for age groups 5 years and older who have not been proven infected with the Coronavirus.
Is it possible to modify the visiting times for the Rawdah Sharifah?
There is no feature to change visiting times, but you can cancel appointments through the Nusk app and book permits at different times.
